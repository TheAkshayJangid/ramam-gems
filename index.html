infoAbout Ramam Gems

Three Decades of Gemstone Excellence

Founded in 1990 by Murari Lal Sharma in the heart of Jaipur — India's gemstone capital — Ramam Gems has grown from a trusted local shop into a name recognised by buyers across India and around the world.

Every stone we sell is handpicked for colour, clarity and cut, and supplied with an authenticity certificate from a recognised gemological laboratory. From a single ring stone for your family to bulk wholesale lots for jewellers, we treat every customer with the same honesty and care.

  • Founder: Murari Lal Sharma
  • Established: 1990 — 30+ years of excellence
  • Trade: Precious & semi-precious gemstones, wholesale + retail
  • Supply: National & international, insured shipping
  • Quality: 100% natural, lab-certified stones
