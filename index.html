About Ramam Gems

30+ Years of

Crafting Trust, One Gem at a Time

Founded with an unwavering passion for authentic gemstones, Ramam Gems has been a cornerstone of the gemstone industry for over three decades. Under the expert guidance of Murari Lal Sharma, we have built an enduring legacy of quality, transparency, and absolute client satisfaction.

Headquartered in the Gem Capital of India — Jaipur, Rajasthan, we specialize in sourcing, trading, and supplying both precious and semi-precious gemstones to discerning clients across India and globally.
